About Me

AI & LLM Engineer & Data Scientist

I'm Zhoutian Xu, a Research Scientist Intern at Alibaba Group focused on Large Language Models and Large-Multimodal-Models for map and navigation. Currently pursuing my Master's in Operations Research and Information Engineering at Cornell University, I combine advanced technical skills in Python, PyTorch, and machine learning with practical experience in data science and AI applications. My academic background includes specialized coursework in Artificial Intelligence, NLP, Deep Learning, and Reinforcement Learning.

My professional journey spans diverse industries including automotive (Tesla, Mercedes-Benz), entertainment (Disney), and retail (Wegmans), where I've developed and implemented data-driven solutions that deliver measurable impact. From creating anomaly detection algorithms and building dynamic time warping models to optimizing pricing strategies and improving queue management, I'm passionate about leveraging technology to solve complex business challenges. Currently, my research focuses on developing algorithm-driven quantization frameworks for LLMs and designing multi-agent cooperation & interaction schemes.
